Frequently
Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our services, therapy, and mental well-being.

How do I book a therapy session? +

You can book a session by clicking “Get Started”, creating an account and choosing a therapist that matches your needs. Available times are shown in real time.

Are online sessions available? +

Yes, all our therapists offer secure video sessions. You can choose online or in-person depending on your preference and location.

What's the difference between therapy and coaching? +

Therapy focuses on mental health, past experiences and emotional healing. Coaching is more goal-oriented and focuses on future actions, habits and personal growth.

Do I need a subscription to access services? +

No subscription is required. You can book individual sessions or packages. Some wellness programs are available as monthly plans.

Can I switch therapists if I don't feel the right connection? +

Absolutely. Finding the right fit is important. You can switch therapists at any time at no extra cost for the first session with a new specialist.

Is my information and session history kept confidential? +

Yes. All data is encrypted and stored according to strict privacy standards. We never share your information without your explicit consent.
